Palestinian culture, built to last.

01 / Approach

Support that goes deeper than advice.

We move beyond short-term interventions. Mada builds lasting strength inside cultural institutions, then connects them to expertise, funders and one another.

01

Fundraising readiness

Skills, tools and networks to pitch projects well, and earned-income models that reduce dependence on donors.

02

Organizational resilience

Stronger governance, leadership succession and structures, with a culture of reflection, adaptation and agility.

03

Long-term sustainability

Real resources, specialists and hands-on implementation behind every strategic recommendation.

04

A connected ecosystem

Shared learning, resource exchange and bridges to global partners, across disciplines and regions.

02 / Model

Where the effort goes.
